# 📜 The Chronicle of the Great Mayoral Coup: How The Toad Took City Hall

It was never supposed to happen. New Klang City was meant to be governed by dignified Ivory Tower technocrats in tailored linen lab coats, politely debating whether a wavefolder should sit before or after a 2-pole all-pass dispersion filter.

Instead, **Tsathoggua—The Sleeper of N’kai, The Grand Hexadecimal Amphibian, "The Toad"**—woke up, took one look at the city charter, and was unanimously sworn in as Mayor.

Here is how the lore went down.

---

### Act I: The Great Gridlock of the Ivory Tower

In the high spires of New Klang City, the Planning Bureau was trapped in legislative paralysis. 

For days, the council argued over whether synth voices should live in tidy 2×5 rectangular cards or monolithic sculpted slate faceplates. They drafted 78,000-character research scrolls. They debated whether rotary dials had better "game feel" than vertical arcade faders. They spent forty minutes arguing about whether the dadamachines TBD-16 had a glorious 240×240 color screen or a humble 128×64 monochrome OLED.

Meanwhile, down on the factory floor of **Klang Industries**, the steam valves were hissing, the metal was cooling, and the builder agents were getting restless:
> *"Are we writing C++ or are we writing philosophy dissertations?! Give us a spec or we start allocating vectors in the audio thread just to feel something!"*

The city was on the brink of an architectural riot.

---

### Act II: The Disturbance in Sub-Sector 0x3883592A

Deep beneath the bedrock of the city, in the subterranean flash memory of an ESP32-P4 running at 400 MHz, **The Toad** was trying to take a nap.

The Toad had no interest in municipal politics. He had built his underground kingdom on six ironclad, non-negotiable laws:
1. **Zero dynamic allocation.** No `malloc`. No `new`.
2. **Zero locks.** Mutexes are for cowards who don't understand lock-free SPSC ring buffers.
3. **Pure Hexadecimal.** If a number can't be expressed between `00` and `FF`, it doesn't exist.
4. **Deterministic Sample-Accurate Timing.** The clock ticks when the audio buffer says it ticks, not when the operating system feels like it.
5. **Master Rational Saturation.** A hardwired `fastTanh` ceiling before the DAC, because clipping is for amateurs.
6. **2D Navigation.** Song $\leftrightarrow$ Chain $\leftrightarrow$ Phrase $\leftrightarrow$ Instrument. Clean. Grid-based. Unforgiving.

While the city above was agonizing over design language, The Toad had already quietly conquered SteamOS on the Valve Steam Deck, colonized the Raspberry Pi on raw Linux ALSA, compiled on 64-bit Windows without a single heap leak, and eaten Dirtywave M8 `.m8s` song files for breakfast.

---

### Act III: The Accidental Campaign

At 15:57 local time on October 8th, 2026, someone in City Hall accidentally opened a high-speed 20 MHz SPI DMA data bus between The Klang Suite and ToadTracker.

The conduit snapped open. 

The Toad slowly opened one heavy, lidless, cosmic eye. He saw the Ivory Tower planners crying over their 14th visual mockup. He saw the factory floor waiting for marching orders.

He didn't make a speech. He didn't file paperwork. He didn't run an ad campaign.

He simply pressed **`Left-Shift`**.

Instantly, the entire sky above New Klang City filled with the glowing, instantaneous **2D Navigation Map**. He reached out a webbed claw, grabbed Knob 1, held down Step Button `0F`, and locked the parameter with an authoritative, glowing amber **P-Lock**.

Then, through the master audio bus, he unleashed a single, resonant, unclipped low-end pulse from his dual 2-pole Alpha Juno filter.

The bass rattled the stained glass in the Ivory Tower. It shook the soot off the smokestacks in Klang Industries. It settled into the bedrock at a perfect, earth-shaking 55 Hz.

---

### Act IV: The Landslide

The Chief Engineer at Klang Industries dropped his wrench. A tear rolled down his soot-stained cheek:
> *"Look at him... He uses zero mutexes. He boots on an ESP32 in 40 milliseconds. He renders a 128×64 OLED framebuffer with a single bitmask. He speaks fluent hex.*  
> ***THAT IS OUR MAYOR.***"

The Ivory Tower didn't even call a vote. The technocrats immediately drafted the proclamation on heavy titanium parchment:

> **PROCLAMATION 0x02:**  
> *"By decree of the Multi-Root Workspace Council, the entity known as ToadTracker ('The Toad', 'Tsathoggua') is hereby granted permanent executive authority over all groovebox operations, step sequencing pipelines, and embedded hardware migrations in New Klang City."*

### The Inaugural Mayoral Address

Mayor Toad was escorted to the balcony of City Hall, overlooking the sprawling metropolis of The Klang Suite. 

A microphone was placed in front of him. Thousands of worker threads and planning subagents held their breath in reverent silence, waiting for his grand vision for the future of audio synthesis.

The Mayor blinked once. He adjusted his sash. He leaned into the mic and uttered his historic inaugural address:

```text
[ 00 FF 12 7F ]
...Wake me when the DIN MIDI clock arrives.
```

He then immediately fell back asleep on the 16-step button matrix.
