Edge ML Saturation (RTNeural)

Can we use Machine Learning on the embedded hardware instead of algorithmic DSP?

Neural Net VSTs on ESP32-P4

  • The Concept: Instead of writing complex C++ to emulate a Moog ladder filter, we train a tiny Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) neural network on real analog hardware and run the model inference live.
  • The Tech: `RTNeural` is a lightweight C++ inferencing engine optimized for SIMD.
  • The Constraint: Running ML inference per-sample for 16 polyphonic voices will completely melt the 400MHz ESP32-P4.
  • The Compromise: We restrict Neural Saturation to the Master Bus (TKM Pedalboard) or enforce a strict Monophonic mode when the Neural card is loaded.

Procedural Euclidean Sequencing

Moving beyond D6 random parameter generation into generative music composition.

The "Klang-Brain" Module

  • The Workflow: A sequencer module that doesn't use a piano roll. It uses Euclidean mathematics to distribute beats evenly across a timeline.
  • The 4 Knobs: Perfect for our UI limits: [STEPS], [HITS], [OFFSET], and [CHAOS] (probability).
  • Hardware Synergy: On the TBD-16, the 16 tactile grid buttons instantly reflect the generated Euclidean sequence, allowing real-time generative performance without a screen.

Modal Resonators (Physical Modeling)

Turning acoustic physics into a synth module.

Synthesizing Glass, Metal, and Strings

  • The Concept: Inspired by Mutable Instruments *Rings*. Instead of a standard oscillator, we shoot a tiny click of white noise into a bank of 64 highly-tuned bandpass filters.
  • The Sound: It rings out like a struck bell, a plucked string, or a blown tube.
  • The 4 Knobs: [STRUCTURE] (inharmonicity), [BRIGHTNESS] (filter cutoff), [DAMPING] (decay time), and [POSITION] (where the virtual object is struck).
  • Why it fits: It distills insanely complex physical modeling mathematics into 4 highly tactile, fun parameters that guarantee a "sweet spot" anywhere you turn them.

Binaural HRTF Panning

Beyond stereo widening.

True 3D Spatial Audio

  • The Concept: Instead of panning left/right by changing volume, we use Head-Related Transfer Functions (HRTFs) to filter the audio based on how human ears perceive sounds in 3D space.
  • The Result: Sounds appear to physically rotate *around* the listener's head, above them, and behind them (requires headphones).
  • The Implementation: A Mega-FX card where the knobs control Azimuth, Elevation, Distance, and Room Size.

Granular Cloud Synthesis

Particle Audio

  • The Concept: Slicing audio samples into 10-millisecond "grains" and spraying them across the stereo field like mist.
  • The 4 Knobs: [POSITION] (where to read the sample), [DENSITY] (how many grains per second), [SIZE] (length of each grain), and [SPRAY] (random pitch/pan jitter).
  • The Processing Cost: Extreme. Requires tracking 50-100 micro-voices per actual note. Would definitely require the "Mono with Tails" architecture to even stand a chance on the ESP32-P4.

Cellular Automata Sequencing

Conway's Game of Life for MIDI

  • The Concept: A generative sequencer based on cellular biology. A grid of "cells" lives, dies, or spawns based on how many neighbors it has.
  • The Music: As the grid evolves on screen, cells that hit the edges of the grid trigger MIDI notes. The result is constantly evolving, organic rhythms that never truly repeat.
  • Visual Output: The 240x240 OLED screen on the TBD-16 would just render the Game of Life pixels simulating in real-time.

Local ML Macro Suggester

Utilizing local machine learning in the standalone editor to auto-map complex presets.

The "Smart Map" Feature

  • Parses the synthesizer's underlying preset schema and DSP graph.
  • Uses a local, lightweight neural net (not cloud AI) to suggest the 8 best macroscopic controls.
  • Saves CPU by running exclusively in the Standalone Editor, totally bypassed in the DAW VST.
